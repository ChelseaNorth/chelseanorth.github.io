Sarah's Ending

Sarah goes out the window first, slowly. As she makes her way out, the door breaks open! The zombies are climbing over and pushing past the barricade.

Your classmate Joe gets attacked first and he screams for help while getting eaten alive.

“Come on!!” Sarah yells.

You classmates rush to Joe’s side while you climb out the window. You land safely outside.

You hear the screaming coming from the classroom, and you and Sarah see a herd of zombies rush in the building to follow the noise.

“We made it out!” Sarah exclaims.

You don’t answer. You both know what happened to everyone inside the classroom.

ENDING: You decided to put yourself first and escape, at the price of your classmates lives. But, you survived!
