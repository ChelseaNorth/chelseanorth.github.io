You look through the door crack and discover someone half eaten at the door. And roaming around the halls are zombies!

Your classmates start arguing about what to do and getting loud. All this noise attracts zombies, and they start banging and scratching the door. The barricade can’t hold for much longer.

Your classmate, Sarah, comes to you and whispers a plan! To sneak out the window with her while everyone is distracted arguing.

Do you stay and try to help or sneak out?
