Hero Ending

You turn back to get Joe. You swing your Owala across the zombie’s skull. The zombie lets go of Joe but it wasn’t enough to kill it...

Joe scrambles to his feet, quickly picking up his textbook.

He rushes the zombie and smashes it’s head with the textbook over and over again.

The zombie finally stops moving, and you and Joe are safe... for now.

The two of you run after the others. You make it down the stairs to the exit at the end of the hall.

Since the first time the outbreak started, you stop running. You look around and realize your whole class made it out!

Perfect ending: Everybody made it out! But where do we go now...
