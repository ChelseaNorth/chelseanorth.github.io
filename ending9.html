Johnny’s Ending

You reach the entrance of your dorm’s building.

The front door is barricaded and locked. You knock on the glass, hoping someone inside will hear you.

After a few moments, the door opens, and another student pulls you inside before quickly re-securing the entrance.

They ask if you're okay. You nod, still catching your breath.

Upstairs, you find several other students gathered in the second floor lounge.

You spend the night working together to secure the lounge and finding supplies.

ENDING: You chose to help someone when you could have kept running. In the end, you found a community. You all are safe, for now...
