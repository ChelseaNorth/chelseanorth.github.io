OUR STORY

Welcome to La Chelita

In the 1970s, Graciela "Chelita" Ramírez opened a small tortillería and began feeding her neighbors. They were families coming off long shifts who wanted a hot plate that tasted like home. She pressed tortillas by hand every morning and cooked the Jalisco dishes she knew by heart.

Today, her granddaughter, Jessica, runs the kitchen.
