Our Story

A table for everyone.

In the 1970s, Graciela “Chelita” Ramírez opened a small tortillería and began feeding her neighbors. They were families coming off long shifts who wanted a hot plate that tasted like home. She pressed tortillas by hand every morning and cooked the Jalisco dishes she knew by heart. When a family couldn’t pay that day, she wrote the amount on a tab and told them it could wait. On birthdays, she pushed the tables together, and the whole block came.

Today her granddaughter, Jessica, runs the kitchen. Jessica grew up Mexican-American in Texas, with her abuela’s cooking and Texas food on the same table, and she cooks with both. The tortillas and the family recipes stay just as Chelita made them. Jessica adds her own dishes, like a vegetarian plate and weekend brunch, so neighbors who have eaten here for decades and neighbors who moved in last year can share a room. The street looks different now, but the door is still open!
