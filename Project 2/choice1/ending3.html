No Man Left Behind Ending

You ignore Sarah’s selfish plan. You bang on the desk, getting everyone’s attention.

You tell everyone the plan to escape out the window. Sarah looks at you, clearly frustrated.

Everyone rushes to the window. Only one person can escape out the window at a time, so, one by one, we climb out the window.

Right as its your turn to climb out the window, the zombies breaks down the door! They’re climbing and pushing through the barricade.

The remainder of everyone waiting to get out the window panics and tries to push you out the way, so they could escape.

You all hear a scream, only to turn around and see Sarah getting ripped to shreds. She disappears into the crowd of zombies.

You use the distraction to your benefit and rush out the window.

You hit the ground and see a handful of your classmates that were able to escape out the window. Behind you, the classroom is filled with fighting over who gets out the window first, which is soon turned into screaming.
