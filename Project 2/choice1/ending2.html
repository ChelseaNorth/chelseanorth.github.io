Joe's Ending

You decide its too risky to stay inside. You and your class prepare by finding weapons, which there’s not many in a classroom. You grab your Owala water bottle and your classmates grab other miscellaneous items like books and pencils.

You guys start moving the desks from the door and prepare to attack. Joe counts down for the attack... “Three. Two. One!”

The door flies open and the zombies rush towards us. You swing your Owala at the closest zombie as hard as you can, knocking it back so you can run. Your class follows Joe’s lead and rushes down the hallway toward the exit.

You push through the exit and for a moment you thought the plan went perfectly. You turn around and realize not everyone it. You see the blood trail leading down the hallway, and you hear the screams of your classmates.

ENDING: You and Joe escaped alive, but your classmates died a gruesome death.
