Karma Ending

The front door is barricaded and locked. You knock on the glass, hoping someone inside will hear you.

Someone comes out. They're standing near the entrance, visibly shaken.

"You just left them there," they say.

You reply, confused, “Huh?”

“I saw you!” They scream.

Before you can respond, they shove you hard. You stumble backward, losing your footing just as an infected comes around the corner.

You try to catch yourself, but it's too late. The infected grabs you before you can get back up.

The person watches for a moment, then rushes inside and locks the door.

ENDING: You were left to die, just as you did to someone else.
