Christopher's Ending

30 minutes go by and you still hear footsteps from the zombies. Christopher is sitting on the floor, everyone assuming he’s scared and tired from running.

Out of nowhere, his breathing becomes heavier and his eyes turn glassy and white. He starts shaking and collapses on the floor straight on his face.

Everyone rushes towards him. You start shaking him but get no response. Joe tries to move him on his back, and suddenly, Christopher’s eyes widen. Christopher lunges at Joe, trying to bite him!

The classroom erupts into chaos. The desks are knocked over as everyone tries to get away, but the main door is barricaded.

You realized the truth too late... One by one, your classmates are eaten alive. You try to run but Christopher catches you.

ENDING: Christopher was bitten in his classroom. By letting him in, you unknowingly brought a zombie inside. Your classroom was overrun and you died a gruesome death.
