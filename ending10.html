Safe Haven Ending

You and Taylor reach the entrance of the dorm’s building.

The front door is barricaded and locked. Taylor knocked on the door three times slowly, maybe some sort of code?

After a few moments, the door opens, and another student pulls you inside before quickly re-securing the entrance.

Upstairs, you find several other students gathered in the second floor lounge.

You spend the night working together to secure the lounge and finding supplies.

ENDING: In the end, you found a community. You all are safe, for now...
